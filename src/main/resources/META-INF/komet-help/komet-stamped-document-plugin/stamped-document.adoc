= The STAMPed Document card

The STAMPed Document card shows an AsciiDoc document that is kept in git, as the knowledge base holds it.
Every line carries the STAMP of the commit that wrote it: who wrote it, when, and on which path.
You can read the document, see where each line came from, review lines, and edit it.

To show your first document, see xref:opening-a-document.adoc[Opening an AsciiDoc document].

== The toolbar

From left to right:

Document chooser:: The documents the knowledge base holds. Choose one to show it. It says _No documents_ until a document has been opened.
Views:: How the document is shown. _Source_ shows it as lines, each with its number, the STAMP that introduced it and any annotation mark. A STAMP's identicon is drawn on the first line of each run of lines it introduced, and a bar in its colour runs down the rest of the run. Lines wider than the card scroll sideways together, with one scrollbar at the bottom of the card. _Reading_ shows it as a document, with each block's date in the left margin and its STAMPs in the right. A date or a STAMP is drawn where it differs from the block above; point at a block to see the ones it shares with the block above. In both views the header shows the date and the STAMPs of whatever is at the top of the card. _Page layout_ shows it on pages, as Print to PDF makes them. _Graph_ shows it as the knowledge base holds it: its sections and blocks, their lines and the references in them, each with its STAMP. A file the document shows in two places, whole in one and by a tagged passage in the other, stands in the graph under each include line that shows it. The one shown by a tagged passage says its tag, as in _tag=brief_, and a row selects the lines in the place where that row stands.
Modes:: Shown with the Graph view only. _Latest_ shows the document as it stands. _History_ adds each line's earlier versions and the lines later commits retired. _Elements_ shows it as elements, the way a structure editor shows a document: a block's markup lines are folded into its title and attributes, and a paragraph's lines are joined into text, sentences and inline elements. It shows the document as it reads: the lines an `ifdef` or `ifndef` leaves out are not shown there, as they are not in the Reading view, and neither are the `ifdef` and `endif` lines themselves. _Latest_ and _History_ show every line.
Layer:: What is drawn over the text: marginalia, commit tints, the review queue and annotation marks. _Passages left out_ draws, in the Reading view, the passages an `ifdef` or `ifndef` leaves out of the document as it reads: faint, each under a line that says when it is read. With it on, such a passage can be edited there. A paragraph that a conditional parts is edited as its whole source, with the `ifdef` and `endif` lines among its lines.
More:: The verbs that act on the whole document, described below.

When the card is wide, each control shows its name beside its icon. When it is narrow, only the icon shows; hover over it to see its name.

== The More menu

Find…:: Opens a bar under the Repository line for finding text; Command-F opens it from anywhere in the card. The text is found as you type it, whatever its case, from where you are reading: in the Reading view in the text of the blocks and in the captions of figures and tables, in the Source view in the lines as written. Return, or Command-G, goes to the next match and Shift-Return to the one before; a match under a fold opens the fold. Esc closes the bar. The Page layout and the Graph are not read.
Find and Replace…:: Opens the find bar with a second row, as Option-Command-F does: the words to put in place of what is found, Replace and Replace All. Replace changes the match shown and goes to the next; Replace All changes every match, from the last to the first, and puts you back where you were reading. Each replacement is an edit of its block, as a mark is: the bar at the foot of the card counts the lines edited, Commit records them and Discard… takes them back. Words are replaced in paragraphs, list items and admonitions, and anywhere in the Source view; a match in a heading, a caption or a table cell is left as it stands, and the status line says how many were.
Open file…:: Chooses a committed `.adoc` file and shows it, materializing it first if the knowledge base lacks it.
Refresh:: Reads the list of documents and the shown document again.
Export…:: Writes the document from the knowledge base back into a working tree at its recorded path, and compares it with HEAD.
Print to PDF…:: Typesets the document, with the documents it includes, through your installed Typst.
Materialize:: Writes the commits that the knowledge base lacks, of the shown file and of every file it includes.
Rebuild from Git:: Drops the document from the knowledge base and materializes it again. Only ephemeral documents can be rebuilt; see xref:kinds-of-data.adoc[Kinds of data].
Remove Superseded Documents:: Deletes older copies of documents that were materialized again in the current form.
Show history:: Lists the STAMPs of the selected lines, or of the whole document, in order.
Go to page…:: Scrolls the Page layout to a page.
Attributes set for this card:: Lists the attribute names the document's `ifdef` and `ifndef` lines ask about. Check one to read the document as a document that sets it would, in every view and in the PDF: with _compendium_ checked, a book reads as the compendium reads the same topics. The status line says what the card has set, and the card remembers it for that document. A name the document sets itself is shown checked and cannot be changed here.

== Reviewing and editing

Click in a block to give it the focus. The Reading view marks the block with a bar at its left edge, and the caret stands where you clicked.
Drag across words to select them, or double-click one word; Shift-click, or the arrow keys with Shift, select from the caret.

The right-click menu is the same in the text and in the margins beside it. In a margin it acts on the block the margin stands beside, whose lines it shows selected.
Its _Fold_ menu folds the block by what it is, a paragraph, a listing or a figure; the table or list the block is in, from any row or item; and each section around it, the nearest first. _Fold every section_ leaves the outermost headings alone on the page, and _Unfold everything_ opens every fold. A fold is opened by a click on its line, or by _Unfold_ where it was made.
Its _Layers_ menu turns on and off what is drawn over the document, as the toolbar's layer menu does: dates, stamps, comments, annotation marks and the others.

A judgment is recorded on whole lines of the source, because a line is what carries a STAMP.
Right-click and choose _Affirm…_, _Refute…_ or _Comment…_ to record your judgment of the lines your selection touches; with no words selected the menu shows those lines selected.
Shift-click outside your selection to select every line from where it began to the line you click.
A judgment is a commit in the document's repository, signed or unsigned, and stays with the lines until the text they judge changes.

To mark words, select them in the Reading view, in Page layout or in the Source view.
A bar of marks appears where you finished selecting: bold, italic, underline, strike through, monospace, highlight and link.
Each mark has a key, which its button shows when you hover over it, and the same marks are under _Format_ on the right-click menu.
Choosing a mark the words already have removes it.
_Link_ opens the block's source in the Reading view with the address selected, for you to type.
Marks are written in a paragraph, a list item or an admonition.

To change the words themselves, right-click a block in the Reading view and choose _Edit block_.
A mark or an edit is saved at once, and is pending until you commit it.

== Committing your edits

While an edit is pending, a bar shows at the bottom of the card, above the status line.
It says how many lines are edited, for example _3 lines edited_.
When nothing is pending the bar is not there.

Message:: A line that says what the edit is for, such as _Mark the dose bold_. You can leave it empty. The commit then names the document and lists what changed.
Sign:: Signs the commit with your key, so that it can be shown to be yours. The card remembers your choice, and your annotations follow it.
Commit:: Writes the document with your edits into its folder and records them there as your own git commit. Pressing Enter in the message does the same. Nothing is pushed.
Discard…:: Asks whether to discard the edited lines, and says how many there are. If you agree, the document reads as it was last committed.
Restore as pending:: Shows after you discard an edit, or after a commit leaves part of an edit out. It brings that edit back as a pending edit.

When the edits are in more than one document, as with a document and one it includes, each _Commit_ records one document.
The bar stays until every document is committed.

If the document's file was also changed outside Komet, _Commit_ first asks about that file in a dialog, because you choose which changed file the commit holds.
_Commit…_ in the Repository panel opens the same dialog.
